Welcome to the Socrates BSD 9 filesystem.

This volume is a real, writable FAT32 disk. Everything you create
here survives a reboot - and you can even mount disk.img on your
host machine to exchange files with the OS.

Try, from the terminal:
  echo my first file > hi.txt
  cat hi.txt
  mkdir projects
  cp hi.txt projects/copy.txt
  ls projects
  df
